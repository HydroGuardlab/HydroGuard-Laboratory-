About the laboratory

HydroGuard Laboratory is a proposed water quality testing laboratory established in Kabacan, Cotabato. We provide accurate, reliable, and accessible testing services that assess the microbiological, physical, and chemical quality of water from different sources — helping customers identify contamination, promote water safety, protect public health, and support environmental protection.

Products & services

What HydroGuard Laboratory delivers to households, water-refilling stations, schools, healthcare institutions, restaurants, hotels, and businesses.

Products

A detailed report containing the results of the water sample analysis.

Laboratory Analysis Results

Customized Testing Packages

Water Quality Documentation

Documented results customers can use for monitoring, reference, or compliance purposes.

Services

  • Client Assistance

    We assist customers with inquiries, testing requirements, sample preparation, and other concerns, with clear information to help clients choose the right service.

  • Testing and Analysis

    Microbiological, physical, and chemical analysis of submitted water samples, following appropriate laboratory procedures for accurate, reliable results.

  • Sample Handling and Collection

    We accept water samples from customers and may provide collection services, with proper handling, labeling, storage, and transportation to maintain sample quality.

  • Result Reporting

    Customers receive a laboratory report with the information and results of the tests performed, presented clearly for easier understanding and reference.

  • Customized Testing

    Testing options based on the specific needs of different customers — households, businesses, schools, water-refilling stations, and other establishments.

  • Community Water Monitoring

    Regular water quality monitoring that supports communities and establishments in identifying changes or possible contamination in their water sources.

  • Information and Awareness

    Basic information about water quality, proper sample handling, and the importance of regular testing, to promote safer water practices in the community.

Open positions

Five roles are open as we build the founding HydroGuard team. Select a role to read more.

Lead Microbiologist

Leads microbiological testing of water samples, oversees laboratory procedures, and ensures results are accurate and reliable across every sample the lab handles.

Laboratory Technician

Performs microbiological, physical, and chemical analysis of submitted water samples, following proper laboratory procedures to produce dependable results.

Marketing Coordinator

Shares information about water quality and the laboratory's services with households, businesses, schools, and the wider community, and helps build public awareness around safe water practices.

Customer Service

Assists clients with inquiries, testing requirements, and sample preparation, providing clear information so customers can choose the right testing service for their needs.

Field Sample Collector

Collects water samples from customer sites and handles labeling, storage, and transportation so that sample quality is preserved on the way to the laboratory.
